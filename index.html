How I work

From idea to final frame

  1. Idea

    A reference or my own concept. Mood, composition and light are decided before the first render.

  2. Workflow

    Web tools run on instructions I prepared in advance. Locally in ComfyUI I pick the sampler and scheduler for the model and the task.

  3. Variations

    Batches with a seed sweep, or a locked seed and a changing prompt, so only one thing moves at a time.

  4. Selection

    Anatomy, eyes, emotion and composition decide which frames stay.

  5. Finish

    Colour and light correction, then grain and noise shaped until the frame reads as a photograph.

I work as an engineer at the Institute of Physics, NAS of Ukraine, and study optics for my master's degree. Knowing how light and lenses behave is what I lean on for photorealism.
